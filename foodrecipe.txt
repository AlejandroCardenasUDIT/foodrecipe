SPANISH POTATO OMELETTE (TORTILLA DE PATATAS)

Ingredients (serves 4):
- 5 medium potatoes
- 6 eggs
- 1 onion (optional)
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Salt

Preparation:
1. Peel and cut the potatoes into thin slices. If using onion, cut it into thin strips.
2. Heat plenty of oil in a pan over medium heat and add the potatoes (and onion). Fry them slowly, without browning, until soft (about 20-25 min).
3. Drain the oil well from the potatoes.
4. Beat the eggs in a large bowl with a pinch of salt.
5. Mix the drained potatoes with the beaten eggs and let it rest for 5 minutes.
6. Heat a little oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat.
7. Pour in the mixture and set the edges, moving the pan so it doesn't stick.
8. Once it's set on the bottom (2-3 min), flip it using a plate and cook the other side (2-3 more min, depending on how well-done you like the inside).
9. Let it rest for a couple of minutes before cutting.
